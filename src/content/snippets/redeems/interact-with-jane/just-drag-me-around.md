Just drag Jane wherever.

* Click and drag on the screen to pull Jane in that direction.
* Click + shift and drag up or down to grow or shrink Jane.
* Click + shift and drag left or right to rotate Jane.

This redeem integrates with the [GreenHeat](https://heat.prod.kr/) Twitch extension.

**On mobile?**

Look for the GreenHeat icon.
Tap it to reveal an area that you can use to drag Jane around.

_Rotating and resizing Jane is not supported on mobile devices at this time._
